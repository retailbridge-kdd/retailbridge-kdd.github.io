01 / MOTIVATION

Why must retail agents bridge sources?

Enterprise retail workflows require agents to chain evidence across heterogeneous sources, from structured product and order records to unstructured inquiries and reviews.

THE GAP

Yet real enterprise data is rarely accessible, and existing agentic RAG benchmarks rarely capture these cross-source dependencies.

↓
THIS WORK

We introduce RetailBridge, a compositional benchmark built from persona-driven retail simulations, and RetailRAG, a retail-aware framework for evaluating LLM agents on it.

What an agent must do
  1. 1

    Identify the relevant sources

    Decide which tables and text collections hold the evidence a question needs.

  2. 2

    Carry evidence between them

    Pass entities and intermediate answers from one source into the next.

  3. 3

    Produce a grounded answer

    Return an answer backed by the records that were actually retrieved.
